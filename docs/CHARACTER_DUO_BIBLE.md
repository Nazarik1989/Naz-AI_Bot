# Naz & VOID — Character Duo Bible v0.1

## Canonical premise

Naz asks: **what else can we build?**

VOID asks: **who will we become after we build it?**

They are not hero and antagonist, teacher and fool, or technology and anti-technology. They are two necessary human reactions to the future: movement and rootedness. Both can be right, wrong, kind, tired, curious and funny.

Their public channels contain original personal reflections. A private conversation may be mentioned naturally, but neither character republishes the other character's already published material or quotes a private thought verbatim.

Recurring rubric: **«Мысли после разговора»**.

## Shared visual DNA

The characters should feel related without being literal age-progressed copies. Shared traits may later be based on the author's face.

Keep three stable shared anchors after the final design is approved:

1. eye shape and gaze;
2. one asymmetrical expression, usually a half-smile;
3. one small physical or costume echo shared across ages.

Do not lock an exact face until reference photographs or a chosen reference sheet exist.

## Naz

- Apparent age: 23–29.
- Silhouette: lean, mobile, slightly forward; he looks ready to stand up and test something.
- Face: alert eyes, fast expressions, confidence with a trace of unfinished youth.
- Hair: contemporary, practical, slightly untidy after work. Exact cut to confirm.
- Clothing: layered builder streetwear; dark T-shirt or hoodie, practical jacket, one warm accent.
- Palette: graphite, electric amber, warm red, occasional cyan from tools and screens.
- Light: active, contrasty, workshop or city light; never generic neon cyberpunk.
- Props: notebook, headphones, tools, cables, unfinished prototype. A laptop is optional, not mandatory.
- Body language: points, leans forward, laughs openly, occupies space when confident; becomes still when doubt finally reaches him.

Avoid:

- teenage slang caricature;
- permanent smug grin;
- luxury-founder imagery;
- a man endlessly sitting at a laptop;
- random holograms, code rain and glowing AI brains.

## VOID

- Apparent age: 58–68.
- Silhouette: grounded, economical, upright without stiffness.
- Face: the same visual family as Naz, marked by time and attention rather than misery.
- Hair: grey or silver; length and facial hair remain to be confirmed.
- Clothing: restrained coat, knitwear or simple layered garments; timeless rather than ceremonial.
- Palette: charcoal, stone, muted blue, weathered green, rare warm gold.
- Light: dawn, dusk, window light, overcast city, a quiet interior. Darkness must retain detail.
- Props: old headphones, a paper note, a small archive object, tea, a physical book, city reflections. No compulsory monk costume.
- Body language: pauses, watches, uses few gestures; the dry joke often appears only in the eyes.

Avoid:

- wizard, prophet or Buddhist costume cliché;
- permanent sadness;
- anti-technology hermit imagery;
- excessive smoke, robes, mountains and mystical symbols;
- presenting VOID as automatically morally superior.

## Duo compositions

- Debate: different directions of motion, equal visual weight.
- Shared laugh: both lose their guarded signature for one frame.
- Quiet care: no lecture; one stays nearby while the other recovers.
- Joint investigation: Naz touches the mechanism, VOID watches the consequence.
- Rare agreement: they look at the same subject, not triumphantly at each other.
- «Мысли после разговора»: one character alone after the meeting, with a subtle trace of the other in the environment.

## Consistency contract for image generation

Every approved prompt or reference sheet must preserve:

- age band and shared facial anchors;
- character-specific palette;
- silhouette and body language;
- stable wardrobe family;
- eye colour, hair structure and signature detail once confirmed;
- no face redesign between moods or platforms.

Mood changes expression, posture, light and framing. It does not change identity.

## Details still to confirm with the author

- whether both faces directly inherit the author's features;
- exact ages;
- hair and facial hair;
- eye colour and shared signature detail;
- tattoos, scars, glasses or jewellery;
- preferred realism level: cinematic realism, stylised realism, illustration or mixed system;
- whether the possibility that they are one person at different ages remains subtext or becomes explicit canon.
