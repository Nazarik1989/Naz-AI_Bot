# Gaming vertical

The gaming direction is an editorial layer, not a generic gaming-news feed.

## Character roles

- **Naz** owns roughly 70% of the motion: mechanics, experiments, mods, AI tools,
  patch interpretation, sharp humor, and small builder prototypes.
- **VOID** owns roughly 30% of the reflection: attention loops, virtual identity,
  friendship, loneliness, monetization, atmosphere, and what remains after play.
- Their private conversation may seed two original public reflections, but neither
  character republishes the other one's text.

## Commands

```text
/gaming topic
/gaming_commercial topic
/gaming_plan topic
```

`/gaming` creates a manual draft. `/gaming_commercial` adds one restrained product
angle. `/gaming_plan` previews the selected rubric and format without calling a model.
Gaming autopublishing is intentionally disabled until several drafts are reviewed.

## Evidence rule

The characters must not claim that they played, tested, bought, won, or lost unless
the input contains a real gameplay note, clip, screenshot, log, or owner's account of
the experience. Without evidence they analyze, hypothesize, or invite discussion.

## Commercial boundary

Allowed directions:

- original skins, models, textures, overlays, and creator assets;
- community bots, analytics, clan tools, and player utilities;
- official creator ecosystems and disclosed affiliate experiments;
- polls, waitlists, and prototypes that test demand honestly.

Excluded directions:

- sale or transfer of game accounts and credentials;
- grey-market skin custody or promises of investment returns;
- invented prices, patch notes, personal experience, or product demand;
- advertising disguised as an independent character opinion.

Hockey remains a future, separate editorial vertical rather than a gaming subtopic.
